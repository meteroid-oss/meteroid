title: Change invoices wait for your review
date: 2026-11-27
badge: improvement
summary: On subscriptions that don't auto-advance their invoices, plan changes and amendments now leave their invoice as a draft.
---

When a subscription doesn't auto-advance its invoices, its recurring invoices already stayed
drafts until you finalized them. The invoice a change makes now does the same: a plan change, an
amendment, a seat bought or removed, an add-on attached or removed. Nothing is emailed or
collected until you finalize it.

- Changes already scheduled when this ships follow the same rule when they apply.
- A change paid through a checkout is still finalized when the checkout completes.
- Credit notes for unused time are still issued at the change; their credit stays on the
  customer's balance until the next invoice is finalized.
- A change invoice can't be deleted, since what it bills is already in effect: finalize it,
  then void it to waive what it bills. A seat purchase waiting for its payment can still be
  deleted, and its seats never start.

Subscriptions that auto-advance their invoices are unchanged.
